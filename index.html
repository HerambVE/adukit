Be a Food Safety Detective with Our 6-Reagent Kit!

Hi! We are a team of student innovators who wanted to know if the milk, spices, oils, and vegetables we eat at home are truly pure. 🚨 Why We Started This Project

Hidden adulterants like starch, chalk powder, brick dust, and toxic dyes can fool our eyes. Simple 2-minute science tests keep every kitchen safe!

Component #1 Tincture of Iodine Solution
📖 Paper Manual: Page 2

Detection of Starch in Milk, Paneer, Khoya & Ghee

🥛 Food Tested: Milk, Paneer, Butter, Ghee ⚠️ Adulterant: Starch / Mashed Potato

📋 Testing Method (Step-by-Step):

  1. Take 2–3 ml of milk (or ½ teaspoon of ghee/butter/paneer boiled with water and cooled) in the test tube.
  2. Open Bottle #1 (Iodine Solution) using the dropper.
  3. Add 2 to 3 drops of Tincture of Iodine into the sample and gently swirl.
  4. Observe the color change inside the tube after 10 seconds.
✅ Pure Sample

Remains milky white or turns a faint yellowish-brown (natural color of iodine drops).

❌ Adulterated Sample

Turns Dark Blue or Bluish-Black immediately, proving starch was added!

Component #2 Dilute Acid Indicator (HCl / Citric Acid Reagent)
📖 Paper Manual: Page 3

Detection of Metanil Yellow in Turmeric & Chalk in Sugar

🫚 Food Tested: Turmeric Powder, Arhar Dal, Sugar ⚠️ Adulterant: Metanil Yellow Dye / Chalk / Washing Soda

📋 Testing Method (Step-by-Step):

  1. Take ½ teaspoon of turmeric powder or sugar in a clean test tube with 5 ml of water.
  2. Add 5 drops from Bottle #2 (Dilute Acid Reagent) slowly down the side of the tube.
  3. For turmeric: watch for a pink/magenta color change. For sugar/jaggery: watch for fizzing bubbles!
✅ Pure Sample

Turmeric stays natural yellow with no magenta color; sugar dissolves quietly with no fizzing.

❌ Adulterated Sample

Turns Pink/Violet/Magenta (Metanil Yellow dye) or produces fizzy bubbles (chalk/soda).

Component #3 Slaked Lime Reagent (Soaked Chuna)
📖 Paper Manual: Page 4

Detection of Turmeric Coating in Sella Rice & Coal Tar Dye in Tea

🍚 Food Tested: Golden Sella Rice, Tea Leaves/Dust ⚠️ Adulterant: Chemical Turmeric Polish / Coal Tar Dye

📋 Testing Method (Step-by-Step):

  1. Place 1 teaspoon of Sella rice grains (or a pinch of tea dust) on a white glass plate or tile.
  2. Sprinkle a small amount of Component #3 (Soaked Slaked Lime / Chuna) over the sample.
  3. Wait 1 minute and observe the color around the rice grains or on the white lime paste.
✅ Pure Sample

Rice grains do not turn red; genuine tea only shows a faint greenish-yellow chlorophyll tint after some time.

❌ Adulterated Sample

Forms Bright Red or Orange streaks immediately on contact with the slaked lime!

Component #4 Alkaline Milk Indicator (Phenolphthalein / Rosolic Solution)
📖 Paper Manual: Page 5

Detection of Synthetic Detergent & Neutralizers in Milk

🥛 Food Tested: Liquid Milk ⚠️ Adulterant: Detergent Powder, Caustic Soda, Baking Soda

📋 Testing Method (Step-by-Step):

  1. Pour 5 ml of milk into the test tube and add an equal amount (5 ml) of water.
  2. Shake thoroughly for 15 seconds to check for thick soapy lather.
  3. Add 3–4 drops of Bottle #4 (Milk pH Indicator) to confirm alkaline detergent or soda adulteration.
✅ Pure Sample

Forms only a very thin natural foam layer that disappears quickly and stays milky white.

❌ Adulterated Sample

Forms dense soapy lather and turns Pinkish-Red when the indicator is added.

Component #5 Mineral/Vegetable Oil Solvent & White Blotting Swabs
📖 Paper Manual: Page 6

Detection of Malachite Green on Vegetables & Rhodamine B on Sweet Potato/Ragi

🥦 Food Tested: Bitter Gourd, Green Chilli, Peas, Sweet Potato, Ragi ⚠️ Adulterant: Malachite Green & Rhodamine B Chemical Dyes

📋 Testing Method (Step-by-Step):

  1. Take a white cotton swab or blotting strip from the kit and soak it with a few drops of Bottle #5 (Testing Oil Solvent).
  2. Firmly rub the outer green surface of a bitter gourd/green chilli, or the red skin of a sweet potato/ragi.
  3. Inspect the white cotton swab under bright light.
✅ Pure Sample

The white cotton swab remains clean and does not pick up any artificial green or bright pink dye.

❌ Adulterated Sample

Cotton turns Bright Green (Malachite Green) or Reddish-Pink (Rhodamine B).

Component #6 Rectified Spirit Solution & High-Strength Test Magnet
📖 Paper Manual: Page 7

Detection of Papaya Seeds / Light Berries in Black Pepper & Iron Filings in Tea

🌶️ Food Tested: Whole Black Pepper, Tea Leaves, Suji (Rawa) ⚠️ Adulterant: Papaya Seeds, Light Berries & Iron Filings

📋 Testing Method (Step-by-Step):

  1. Black Pepper Test: Drop 10–15 black peppercorns into a tube of water or Bottle #6 (Rectified Spirit). Mature pepper sinks while papaya seeds/light berries float!
  2. Tea / Rawa Magnet Test: Spread 1 spoon of tea leaves or suji on a flat paper plate and sweep the Kit Magnet right over the surface.
✅ Pure Sample

All genuine black peppercorns sink to the bottom; the magnet stays completely clean over tea leaves.

❌ Adulterated Sample

Papaya seeds & hollow berries float on top; tiny black iron filings stick to the magnet!

Common Adulteration in Other Everyday Foods

Adulteration also happens often in everyday kitchen items that don't even require chemical reagents to check! Try these 9 rapid household tests and check them off as you go.

Milk & Dairy

1. Water Added to Milk (Slanting Surface Test)

Adulterant: Added Water
  • Put a single drop of milk on a polished slanting surface (like a clean steel plate).
  • Watch how fast the drop slides down.
✅ Pure: Flows slowly, leaving a clear white trail behind.
❌ Adulterated: Flows down immediately without leaving a white mark.
Oils & Fats

2. Other Cheap Oils Mixed in Coconut Oil

Adulterant: Cheap Refined / Mineral Oils
  • Pour coconut oil into a transparent glass tumbler.
  • Place it in the refrigerator (not the freezer!) for 30 minutes.
✅ Pure: Coconut oil solidifies completely into one uniform white block.
❌ Adulterated: Other oils remain liquid as a separate top layer!
Sugar & Confectionery

3. Sugar Syrup Mixed in Honey

Adulterant: Water & Invert Sugar Syrup
  • Take a transparent glass of water and add one drop of honey from a spoon.
  • Bonus check: Dip a cotton wick in honey and light it with a match (with adult help).
✅ Pure: Drop settles to the bottom as a thread without dissolving; burns cleanly.
❌ Adulterated: Disperses in water right away; crackles or won't burn.
Sugar & Confectionery

4. Aluminium Foil in Silver Leaf (Sweets Vark)

Adulterant: Hazardous Aluminium Leaf
  • Take a small piece of the silver leaf from a sweet and gently crush it between two fingers.
✅ Pure Silver: Crumbles easily into a fine, delicate powder.
❌ Aluminium: Tears into tough metallic shreds instead of powdering.
Salt, Spices & Condiments

5. Brick Powder, Sawdust & Dye in Chilli Powder

Adulterant: Brick Dust, Sawdust & Synthetic Red Dye
  • Sprinkle 1 teaspoon of red chilli powder on the surface of a glass of water.
  • Wait 2 minutes and check the surface, water color, and bottom sediment.
✅ Pure: Settles slowly without immediate red color streaks or floating wood.
❌ Adulterated: Red dye streaks shoot down; sawdust floats; gritty brick dust settles.
Salt, Spices & Condiments

6. Checking Iodine in Kitchen Salt (Potato & Lemon)

Check: Missing Iodine or Chalk in Salt
  • Cut a slice of raw potato, sprinkle salt on the wet surface, and wait 1 minute.
  • Add 2 drops of fresh lemon juice on top.
✅ Iodised Salt: A clear blue color develops on the potato slice!
❌ Non-Iodised: No blue color appears at all.
Salt, Spices & Condiments

7. Grass Seeds Colored with Charcoal in Jeera

Adulterant: Wild Grass Seeds & Charcoal Dust
  • Take a pinch of cumin seeds (jeera) in your palms and rub them firmly together.
✅ Pure: Your palms stay clean and smell naturally aromatic.
❌ Adulterated: Your palms turn black/grey from the charcoal coating!
Beverages

8. Chicory Powder or Clay in Filter Coffee

Adulterant: Chicory Powder & Clay Particles
  • Gently sprinkle 1 teaspoon of coffee powder onto the surface of a glass of cold water.
✅ Pure: Pure coffee powder floats on the surface of the water for a while.
❌ Adulterated: Chicory sinks immediately leaving brown color trails behind.
Fruits, Vegetables & Grains

9. Artificial Green Dye on Green Peas & Coloured Grains

Adulterant: Water-Soluble Synthetic Green/Yellow Dye
  • Place a handful of green peas or yellow dal in a transparent glass of water.
  • Stir well and let it stand for 15–30 minutes.
✅ Pure: Water stays clear with no artificial dye separation.
❌ Adulterated: Water turns bright green or yellow as the dye washes off.
🛡️ Important Notes for Young Scientists

Lab Safety Rules

Always follow these 4 golden rules before opening any reagent bottle from your Food Adulteration Testing Kit!

👩‍🔬

1. Adult Supervision First

Always perform reagent tests with a parent or teacher watching, especially when using Bottle #1 (Iodine) and Bottle #2 (Acid Indicator).

🚫

2. Never Taste Tested Food

Once you add any reagent drop to milk, spices, or sugar, that sample is a science experiment—throw it away safely and wash the tube!

🧤

3. Keep Droppers Separate

Use the matching cap/dropper for each bottle so the 6 reagents don't mix with each other inside the kit box.

🧼

4. Wash Hands After Testing

Rinse test tubes with tap water and wash your hands with soap as soon as you finish your food safety detective work.
